Cube Systems

Cube

Item synthesis system. Feed items to level up the Cube, which unlocks new features and improves crafting. Keep Cube level close to hero level for best efficiency.

🏅 Horroric Cube (Lv10)🏅 Decubed Cain (Lv50)

Alchemy

Transmute weak items into gold. Core part of the farming economy loop.

🏅 Alchemy 101 (1,000 gold)🏅 The Philosopher's Stone (1,000,000 gold)

Decoration

Apply cosmetic and stat-boosting decoration options to equipment. Tiers 1-5+.

🏅 A Decorative Awakening (Tier 1)🏅 Decoration is an Art (Tier 5)

Engraving

Apply engraving options to gear slots for additional stats. Tiers 1-5+.

🏅 Baby Steps in Engraving (Tier 1)🏅 Outshining Jewels (Tier 5)

Inscription

Apply inscription options to weapons or armor. Tiers 1-5+. Can be repeated up to 100 times per item.

🏅 Let's Try Inscribing (Tier 1)🏅 Etched into the Soul (Tier 5)🏅 Obsession? No, Dedication. (100 times)
