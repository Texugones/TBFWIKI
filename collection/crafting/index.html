Explore all Cube operations in one place. Compare unlock levels, crafting materials, rarity probabilities, modifier slots, extraction costs, and Cube experience requirements before spending items or gold.

Synthesis

Combine nine items of the same rarity into one item of the next tier. Items must be of the same grade to be combined.

Decoration

Apply decoration slots to equipment for bonus attributes. Compatible attributes and available materials vary by decoration slot type.

💠

Full recipe data coming soon

Decoration unlocks at Cube Level 8 for 300 Gold. Slot types, compatible attributes, and available materials will be listed here.

Engraving

Engrave equipment with stat attribute ranges. The material tier used determines the range magnitude of the engraved attribute.

✍️

Full recipe data coming soon

Engraving unlocks at Cube Level 15 for 1,000 Gold. Engraving slots and attribute ranges per material tier will be listed here.

Inscription

Inscribe utility attributes onto equipment using layered material combinations. Each inscription layer refines the attribute further.

📜

Full recipe data coming soon

Inscription unlocks at Cube Level 25 for 10,000 Gold. Inscription slots, utility attributes, and material layers will be listed here.

Offering

Spend currency for a chance at randomized rarity reward distributions. Higher investment tiers unlock access to better reward pools.

🎁

Full recipe data coming soon

Offering unlocks at Cube Level 20 for 3,000 Gold. Currency costs and rarity distribution tables will be listed here.

Extraction

Remove applied modifiers from equipment for a gold cost. Extracted modifiers are permanently destroyed — the slot is cleared, not the item.

🔩

Full recipe data coming soon

Extraction unlocks at Cube Level 10 for 1,000 Gold. Gold costs per modifier slot and tier will be listed here.
